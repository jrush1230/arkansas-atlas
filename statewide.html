Arkansas, 2024 to 2026

Where results moved, by county, by education service cooperative, and across all 264 districts.
!
Every level figure here is ADE's own published figure for that county, co-operative, district or the state — not a total built by adding up schools. The three-year change is computed from two of those published figures and is shown only where both years published a figure over 30 tested students; otherwise the reason is printed in place of a number. Spring 2026 is a Pre-Appeals release and can still change. How we handle this.

Three-year change by county

Change in the percentage of students scoring Ready or Exceeding between 2024 and 2026, for each of Arkansas's 75 counties. Every figure is ADE's own County sheet figure; the change between the two is computed from them. A map cannot print a number on every county, so the same 75 counties are listed underneath in order of change — that list, not the shading, is where the figures are.

Every county, in order of change

Three-year change by education service cooperative

The same comparison across the 15 regional co-operatives and Pulaski County Special School District's own service area. Every school belongs to exactly one. Co-operatives are groupings of districts, not of counties, so each area here is its member districts' boundaries combined — it does not follow county lines.

Every co-operative, in order of change

How much districts moved

The distribution of district change, two percentage points to a bar. The spread is the point: a single state figure hides districts moving in opposite directions.

Change against level

Where a district stands in 2026, against how far it moved since 2024. A district low and rising is a different story from one high and falling, and a single ranking cannot tell them apart.
